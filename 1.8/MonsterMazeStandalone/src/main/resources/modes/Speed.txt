MONSTER MAZE - MODE: SPEED
==========================

Modern gameplay with the original timer and monster spawning.

GAMEPLAY
--------
- Uses the original 60-second stage timer, decreasing by 2 seconds per
  stage to a minimum of 15 seconds.
- Uses the original monster spawning progression, starting with 150
  monsters.
- Uses the enhanced kit mechanics available outside Original mode.
- Jumper has 3 charged jumps and reaching a Safe Pad restores the full
  3 charges. Jumping while standing on a Safe Pad is free.
- Maverick is available.
- Slowballer has Cryo Blitz and Body Builder has Body Rush.
- The original "speeding" movement behaviour remains active.

LEADERBOARDS
------------
Personal bests are tracked separately by mode, maze pattern and kit.

PURPOSE
-------
Speed is intended for players who want the enhanced gameplay mechanics
without changing the original timer and monster spawning progression.
