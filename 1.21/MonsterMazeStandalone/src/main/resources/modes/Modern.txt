MONSTER MAZE - MODE: MODERN
===========================

Enhanced gameplay with faster stages, more monsters, and expanded kit abilities.

GAMEPLAY
--------
- Stage timer starts at 35 seconds and decreases toward 15 seconds over
  the first 10 stages, remaining at 15 seconds thereafter.
- 225 starter monsters spawn at the beginning of the run.
- Uses the enhanced kit mechanics available outside Original mode.
- Jumper has 3 charged jumps and reaching a Safe Pad restores the full
  3 charges. Jumping while standing on a Safe Pad is free.
- Maverick is available.
- Slowballer has Cryo Blitz and Body Builder has Body Rush.
- Players whose jumping is locked receive the Modern movement-speed
  adjustment used by the 1.21 implementation.

LEADERBOARDS
------------
Personal bests are tracked separately by mode, maze pattern and kit.

PURPOSE
-------
Modern is the primary enhanced 1.21 gameplay mode. It keeps the core
Monster Maze loop while increasing the pace, monster pressure and kit
options.
